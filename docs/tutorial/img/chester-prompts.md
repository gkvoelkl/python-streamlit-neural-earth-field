# Chester-Bilder: Prompts

Jedes Kapitel beginnt mit einem eigenen Bild von Chester. Erzeugt mit Kling AI (*Image 3.0*, 2K HD, 16:9) und [`chester-referenz.png`](chester-referenz.png) als Referenzbild. Im Repo liegt jeweils eine auf 1600 px Breite verkleinerte JPG-Fassung.

## Kapitel 1 · `kapitel-01-chester.jpg`

Idee: Chester packt die ganze, aus Pixeln bestehende Erde in eine einzige Eichel – das Raster wird zu etwas Kleinem.

```
Use the squirrel from the reference image as the main character: Chester, a fox squirrel
with golden-brown fur, a cream-white belly, big shiny dark eyes and his blue baseball cap.
Keep his look exactly as in the reference, same 3D animated feature film style, cinematic
lighting. Scene: in his futuristic control room at night, Chester stands next to a large
floating hologram of the Earth. The globe is clearly built from thousands of tiny glowing
square pixels, like a coarse digital elevation raster: deep blue oceans, green lowlands,
ochre and brown mountains, white polar caps. A bright, clearly visible stream of these
little square pixels peels off the globe and spirals into a single acorn that Chester
holds up in both paws. The acorn glows warmly from inside, and through its translucent
shell a delicate pattern of luminous lines and tiny nodes is visible, like a miniature
neural network. Chester looks proud and a little mischievous, as if he has just packed
the whole world into one acorn. Wide composition: globe on the left, Chester and the
glowing acorn on the right, both in sharp focus. No text, no letters, no logos besides
his cap.
```

Ausgewählt: Bild 4 von 4 der zweiten Runde.

## Kapitel 2 · `kapitel-02-chester.jpg`

Idee: Das Bild steht vor dem Text, die Leser kennen nur den Titel. Es zeigt deshalb „Training“ und „neuronales Netz“ direkt: Chester trainiert die leuchtende Netz-Eichel aus Kapitel 1. Zwei Referenzbilder: `chester-referenz.png` (Bild 1) und `kapitel-01-chester.jpg` (Bild 2, für die Eichel).

```
Two reference images. Image 1 shows Chester: a fox squirrel with golden-brown fur, a
cream-white belly and big shiny dark eyes. He wears exactly the same cap as in image 1:
a royal-blue baseball cap with a small orange and light-blue infinity logo and the word
CHICAGO in small white letters on the front. Keep Chester's look and the 3D animated
feature film style of image 1, cinematic lighting. Image 2 shows the glowing acorn: a
glossy brown scaly acorn cap with a short stem on a translucent, warm amber-glowing body,
with fine luminous lines and tiny nodes inside like a miniature neural network. The acorn
in the new image must look exactly like the acorn in image 2. Scene: Chester is a sports
coach. A whistle hangs on a cord around his neck, he holds a stopwatch and a clipboard
and watches his trainee with an encouraging, slightly strict look. His trainee is that
glowing acorn, which now has thin cartoon arms and legs, small sneakers and a simple
friendly face with two small eyes and a smile, no glasses and no other accessories. The
acorn works out hard, lifting a small dumbbell above its head, a few sweat drops flying.
The scene is a training corner in Chester's futuristic control room at night, with a
large holographic globe of the Earth glowing softly in the background. Playful, warm,
motivating mood. Wide composition: the acorn in the center-left, Chester on the right,
both in sharp focus. No other text or logos.
```

Ausgewählt: Bild 2 von 4 der zweiten Runde. Erste Runde ohne zweites Referenzbild verworfen: Die Eichel sah anders aus als in Kapitel 1 (teils mit Brille), und die Kappe stimmte nicht.

**Erkenntnis:** Für wiederkehrende Figuren wie die Eichel das Bild des Vorkapitels als zusätzliches Referenzbild mitgeben und die Kappe ausdrücklich beschreiben.

## Kapitel 3 · `kapitel-03-chester.jpg`

Idee: Titel „Architektur eines neuronalen Netzes“. Links das einfachste Modell als einzelnes Brett, in der Mitte ein Gebirge, das die Eichel aus vielen geraden Stäben und Knoten gebaut hat – viele einfache Stücke ergeben eine Form, wie die Knicke der Neuronen. Zwei Referenzbilder: `chester-referenz.png` (Bild 1) und `kapitel-02-chester.jpg` (Bild 2, Eichel mit Ärmchen).

```
Two reference images. Image 1 shows Chester: a fox squirrel with golden-brown fur, a
cream-white belly and big shiny dark eyes. He wears exactly the same cap as in image 1:
a royal-blue baseball cap with a small orange and light-blue infinity logo and the word
CHICAGO in small white letters on the front. Keep Chester's look and the 3D animated
feature film style of image 1, cinematic lighting. Image 2 shows the glowing acorn with
thin cartoon arms and legs and small sneakers: a glossy brown scaly acorn cap with a short
stem on a translucent, warm amber-glowing body with fine luminous lines inside, and a
simple friendly face. The acorn must look exactly like in image 2, no glasses, no other
accessories. Scene: a dark, cozy wooden workshop at night. On the far left, a single plain
straight wooden plank leans clearly visible against the wall: the simplest possible model.
In the center stands a large model of a mountain range that the acorn has built like an
architect: it is made of many thin straight glowing rods joined at small bright glowing
nodes, forming a clear triangular network, like a wireframe or a neural network. The rods
and nodes are clearly visible, with warm orange light, and together they form several
peaks and valleys. The acorn stands at the foot of the mountain model on the left, smiling
proudly. On the right, Chester stands upright as the master architect, holding a small
notepad and a pencil, looking at the model with an approving smile. Warm, playful mood.
Wide composition: plank on the far left, acorn and mountain model in the center, Chester
fully visible on the right, all in sharp focus. No other text or logos.
```

Ausgewählt: Bild 4 von 4 der zweiten Runde. Die erste Runde (Werkstatt im Kontrollraum, Eichel mit letztem Leuchtstab) zeigte entweder das Netz gut oder den Gesamtaufbau gut, nicht beides; der Prompt oben verbindet beides.
